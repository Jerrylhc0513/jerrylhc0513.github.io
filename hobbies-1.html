Hobbies & Life

Collections, craftsmanship, cars, cats, cooking, gaming, music, and film.

Outside research, my interests range from fossils, antiques, and handmade work to cars, cats, cooking, gaming, film and music. My favorite visual motifs include medieval knights, samurai, and cherry blossoms, while black, gold, and yellow are the colors I am most drawn to.

Collections & Handmade Work

I enjoy collecting objects that carry a visible sense of history, particularly fossils, antiques, and paintings. I also enjoy making things by hand, including rugs and ceramic sculptures. These objects connect history, material, and imagination in ways that I find deeply personal.

Favorite Motifs

I am especially drawn to medieval knights, samurai, and cherry blossoms. I like the contrast between strength and restraint, history and transience. Black, gold, and yellow are the colors I most often use as visual accents.

Cars & Driving

Cars are where engineering, design, history, and personality come together. Some of my favorites are the Mercedes-AMG W222 S63, Mercedes-AMG GT R, 1987 Porsche 930 Turbo, Nissan 350Z, Chevrolet Corvette C8, and Series 2 Lotus Exige S.

My Cats

I have three cats, each with a very different personality: Huangdou, an orange tabby; Hudou, an American Shorthair; and Xiaopofu, a Ragdoll. Together, they bring warmth, energy, and plenty of unpredictability into everyday life.

Cooking

Cooking is one of my favorite ways to relax and experiment. I enjoy learning new techniques, recreating dishes that I have tried elsewhere, and sharing food with friends. It combines creativity with the satisfaction of producing something tangible.

Gaming

I enjoy a wide range of video games across most major genres, from competitive shooters and open-world role-playing games to racing, survival horror, strategy, and roguelikes.
