Research on Lightweight Detection Algorithms for Unsafe Passenger Behavior on Escalators

Nan Zhang, Ying Hou, Qilun Li, Haochen Li, Wei Zhang, and Chaoyang Sun.

2026 9th International Conference on Advanced Electronic Technology, Computers and Software Engineering, 2026.

This work studies lightweight deep-learning algorithms for detecting unsafe passenger behaviour in escalator environments. It focuses on balancing detection accuracy, computational efficiency, and deployment feasibility in resource-constrained settings.

Integrating Convexity and DeepLabv3+ for Semantic Segmentation of Power Lines

Ying Hou, Jindou Tuo, Haochen Li, Zhuoxin Yan, Xuemei Huang, and Yuqi Wang.

Research Square Preprint, 2026.

This paper proposes PLE-DeepLabv3+, a lightweight semantic-segmentation model for detecting thin and low-contrast power lines in complex aerial imagery. The model combines a reconfigured ConvNeXtV2 backbone, direction-selective coordinate attention, efficient multi-scale feature fusion, adaptive edge detection, and a class-aware focal loss.

When Does Softmax Attention Outperform Linear Prediction in Nonlinear Time Series?

Haochen Li, Anru Zhang.

Manuscript in preparation for submission.

This work studies when softmax attention can outperform linear prediction in nonlinear time series. It develops a statistical framework that balances the approximation gains of nonlinear attention against the additional costs of model selection and estimation. The theoretical results are accompanied by simulation studies across different signal, noise, and sample-size regimes.

Replication and Cross-Asset Extension of the Intraday Volume Estimator

Haochen Li, Anru Zhang.

Manuscript completed.

This work replicates and extends a Transformer-based probabilistic forecasting framework for intraday sequential data. It examines the model across multiple asset classes and forecast targets, including volume and realized volatility. The analysis considers robustness, causal deployment, and the effect of predictive improvements on downstream decision-making.

2025

Efficient YOLOv11-AntiUAV Algorithm for Counter-UAV Target Detection

Adviser: Assoc. Prof. Ying Hou, Xi’an University of Science and Technology · 2025

Developed an efficient YOLOv11-based framework for detecting small and distant UAV targets under class imbalance. Introduced class-weighted loss design and simulated low-light and motion-blur conditions through data augmentation. Evaluated the model using precision, recall, ROC curves, and confusion matrices.

Lightweight YOLOv11 Network for Multi-Scale Feature Fusion in UAV Small-Object Detection

Adviser: Assoc. Prof. Ying Hou, Xi’an University of Science and Technology · 2025

Developed lightweight YOLOv11 variants by simplifying the feature-pyramid pathway and replacing standard convolutions with grouped convolutions. Analyzed parameter counts, FLOPs, convergence, and speed-accuracy trade-offs across candidate architectures.

Multi-Scale Feature Fusion-Based YOLOv8 Object Detection for UAV Images

Adviser: Assoc. Prof. Ying Hou, Xi’an University of Science and Technology · 2025

Improved multi-scale feature extraction and bounding-box regression for dense small-object detection in aerial imagery. Contributed to data preprocessing, annotation, architectural modifications, and ablation studies evaluated using mAP and recall.
